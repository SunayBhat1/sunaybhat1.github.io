How the committee worked

Timing

    Information diet

    All agents see the same alphabetized candidate list, compact game rows, team résumés, and available ratings. The four conditions cross prior CFP and AP visibility. Poll-off conditions omit the relevant poll table and résumé rank fields. AP helped define the candidate pool, so its influence on eligibility persists across conditions.

    Leakage controls

    The runner verifies the frozen packet SHA-256 and date cutoffs before calling models. Agents receive no search, tools, or retrieval. The input packet predates the target CFP release. Prior CFP publication date is a configured mapping because the source has no timestamp. Prompt instructions cannot erase knowledge already in model weights.

    Ballots and comparisons

    Each ballot ranks exactly 25 unique eligible teams and explains its top 12. A committee needs at least 10 valid ballots. Borda awards 25 points for first through 1 for 25th, breaking ties on available SP+ then alphabetically. Persona contrasts are descriptive.

    Committee types

    Combined committee
    Different AI models fill fixed seats on one committee. Every seat has its own voting style. Model assignments stay the same across the poll settings.
    Combined committee · second vote
    The same models and seats vote again from scratch. Models that support a creativity setting get a higher setting on this vote.
    Single-model committee
    Every seat uses one AI model with different voting styles across seats. Historical runs included these committees to compare how the models rank the same teams.

    The voting styles

    Each voter got a different emphasis, such as efficiency, quality of wins, or head-to-head results.
