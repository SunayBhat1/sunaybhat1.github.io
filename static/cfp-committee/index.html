Study design

Methodology

One frozen evidence packet, four information conditions, and repeatable ballot rules.

Timing

    Information diet

    All agents see the same alphabetized set of 31 candidates, compact game rows, team résumés, and available ratings. The four conditions cross prior CFP and AP visibility. Poll-off conditions omit the relevant poll table and résumé rank fields. AP helped define the candidate pool, so its influence on eligibility persists across conditions.

    Leakage controls

    The runner verifies the frozen packet SHA-256 and date cutoffs before calling models. Agents receive no search, tools, or retrieval. The input packet predates the target CFP release. Prior CFP publication date is a configured mapping because the source has no timestamp. Prompt instructions cannot erase knowledge already in model weights.

    Ballots and comparisons

    Each ballot ranks exactly 25 unique eligible teams and explains its top 12. A committee needs at least 10 valid ballots. Borda awards 25 points for first through 1 for 25th, breaking ties on available SP+ then alphabetically. Persona contrasts are descriptive. The mixed seats are fixed across conditions, and temperature support varies by provider.
